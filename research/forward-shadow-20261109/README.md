# Eight-trade forward Shadow validation

The collection protocol was locked on 9 November 2026 after the application
recorded both selected-Shadow and parallel-Shadow decisions at the 13:10 IST
boundary without a broker-loop error. Because collection began after the
market opened, 9 November is a connectivity and recording soak check and is
excluded from the full-session count. The first eligible full session is
10 November 2026.

The selected model remains research-only. Shadow mode cannot place broker
orders. No model, cutoff, stop, target or risk setting may be selected from
interim results. The current application-wide limit is eight sequential trades
per day with at most one open position.

After at least 20 complete sessions, evaluate all recorded decisions, option
selection audits, quotes, fills and exits. Promotion requires positive net P&L
after charges and under doubled slippage, profit factor of at least 1.30, exact
signal-feature and contract-selection parity, and results that are not driven
by a single day. Prefer at least 100 completed trades; if 20 sessions contain
fewer, continue collection rather than weakening the evidence gate.

`protocol.json` is the frozen machine-readable specification.
